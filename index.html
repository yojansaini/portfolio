RegWatch

New
↗
AI product Regulatory tech Solo build Full stack

An always-on regulatory intelligence platform that crawls RBI and NPCI daily, classifies every circular with an LLM, and pushes the relevant ones to the people who need them. Replaces a manual, research-shaped compliance process with a system. Designed, built and deployed end to end by me, with no engineering support.

The hard part
NPCI renders its circular list client-side, so ordinary scraping returns nothing. Reliable extraction meant working around the rendering layer entirely.
Key decision
Two-tier AI routing: a self-hosted model on EC2 for deep summaries, a free fast API for the ask bot. Calls that don't need depth don't pay for it.
The loop
Every AI tag and severity is surfaced for correction. Fixes pass maker-checker approval, then feed back as training signal.

02

Partial Payments

New
↗
Milestone billing Payments Consent design PRD lead

A three-mode collection feature built for milestone-based billing, where payment only moves when a deliverable is accepted. India has roughly 15 million freelancers and ₹7.34 lakh crore in delayed MSME payments, yet every platform treats collection as a single event or a time-based split. The insight: milestone-linked billing is a contract and consent problem that happens to end in a payment.

The innovation
Milestone mode. A leg moves only when the merchant marks work done and the customer accepts. Disputes loop twice, then escalate to SecurePay mediation.
Hardest decision
Scoping consent inside a merchant-created order. A dedicated consent layer surfaces every fee and penalty upfront, meeting RBI PA 2025 and DPDP 2023.
Architecture
Execution, reconciliation and refund modelled as three independent status chains, so a chargeback on one leg never contaminates the others.
3 collection modes 3 status chains 2 dispute cycles
PRD · Consent design · Edge cases · Mediation flow

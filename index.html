01

RegWatch

Case study ready
↗
AI product Regulatory tech Solo build Full stack

An always-on regulatory intelligence platform that crawls RBI and NPCI daily, classifies every circular with an LLM, and pushes the relevant ones to the people who need them. Replaces a manual, research-shaped compliance process with a system. Designed, built and deployed end to end by me, with no engineering support.

The hard part
NPCI renders its circular list client-side, so ordinary scraping returns nothing. Reliable extraction meant working around the rendering layer entirely.
Key decision
Two-tier AI routing: a self-hosted model on EC2 for deep summaries, a free fast API for the ask bot. Calls that don't need depth don't pay for it.
The loop
Every AI tag and severity is surfaced for correction. Fixes pass maker-checker approval, then feed back as training signal.

02

Partial Payments

Built · write-up in progress
↗
Payments Hosted checkout PRD Edge cases

A multi-mode partial payment capability for SecurePay's hosted checkout, letting a payer settle an invoice in installments or split it across people and methods. The design problem was not the happy path; it was keeping money, order and refund state coherent when a payment is only ever partly complete.

Modes
Installments over time, and splits across multiple payers and payment methods on a single invoice.
Status model
A three-dimension status chain separating payment, order and settlement state, so a part-paid invoice is never ambiguous.
Scope
20+ edge cases specified: overpayment, expiry mid-chain, partial refunds and reconciliation against a part-settled order.
3 status dimensions 20+ edge cases 2 payment modes
